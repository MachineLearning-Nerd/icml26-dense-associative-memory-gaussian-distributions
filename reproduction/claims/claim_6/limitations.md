# Claim 6 limitations and deviations

The paper omits seeds and the exact PSD direction normalization. This
verifier uses three documented seeds and trace-normalized Wishart
directions. It tests the reported one-step effect directly; it does not
infer a universal guarantee from finite simulations.

Figure 8 raw data is unavailable. The prose `1e-3` endpoint and plotted
`1e-6` line are both retained, with the prose value used for the stated
one-step convergence contract.
