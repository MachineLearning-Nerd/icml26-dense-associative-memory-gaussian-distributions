# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

Finite random trials cannot establish the theorem's universal
quantifiers. The claim verdict therefore depends on the independently
reconstructed proof certificate; experiments are calibration and
assumption audits. Polytope samples come from finite-length Gibbs chains,
so uniformity is numerical rather than an exact finite-time draw.
The theorem-sized sweep stops below the conservative contraction `d0`
because an exact nearest-pair calculation at that `N` would be
prohibitively quadratic; the proof certificate covers the asymptotic
step.
