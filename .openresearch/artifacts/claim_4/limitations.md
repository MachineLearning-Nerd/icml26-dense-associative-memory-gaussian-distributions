number of plotted update iterations in prose. We use three deterministic
seeds, isotropic directions, and three updates; one-step convergence is
the reported positive effect. The sphere-radius typo is resolved in
favor of Algorithm 2. Success is the paper's displayed `W2<1e-6`
criterion, not merely nearest-pattern identity.
